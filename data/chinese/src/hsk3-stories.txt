@ hsk3-neighbor-cat | 邻居的猫 | The Neighbor's Cat | story, animals
我 家 楼下 住 着 一 位 老奶奶， 她 养 了 一 只 很 胖 的 橘猫。
An old lady lives downstairs from us, and she keeps a very fat orange cat.
那 只 猫 每天 下午 都 坐 在 门口 晒太阳， 谁 走过去 它 都 不 动。
Every afternoon that cat sits in the doorway sunning itself, and it doesn't move no matter who walks past.
有 一 天 下雨， 它 躲 在 我 的 自行车 下面， 叫 了 一会儿。
One day it rained, and it hid under my bicycle, crying for a while.
我 把 它 抱 回 老奶奶 家， 她 笑 着 给 了 我 一 个 苹果。
I carried it back to the old lady's home, and smiling, she gave me an apple.

@ hsk3-hiking | 爬山 | Climbing the Mountain | story, outdoors
上 个 周末 我 和 朋友 一起 去 爬山。
Last weekend I went hiking with a friend.
山路 不 长， 可是 很 陡， 我们 爬 得 很 慢。
The mountain path was not long, but it was steep, and we climbed very slowly.
爬 到 一半 的时候， 我 已经 累 得 说 不出 话 来。
Halfway up I was already too tired to speak.
朋友 笑 我 说：“ 你 平时 应该 多 运动。”
My friend laughed at me and said: "You should exercise more."
到 了 山顶， 风 很 凉快， 城市 在 下面 变 得 很 小。
At the summit the wind was cool, and the city below had become very small.
那 一 刻 我 觉得， 累 一点 也 没关系。
In that moment I felt that being a little tired didn't matter at all.

@ hsk2-subway | 坐地铁 | Taking the Subway | transport, daily life

Beijing's subway is very convenient, and not too expensive either.
早上 八 点 的时候 人 特别 多， 大家 都 要 去 上班。
At eight in the morning there are especially many people; everyone has to get to work.
我 常常 站 着 看 手机。
I often stand and look at my phone.
有时候 我 会 给 老人 让座。
Sometimes I give up my seat for an elderly person.
从 我 家 到 公司 要 坐 三十 分钟。
From my home to the office takes a thirty minute ride.

@ hsk2-weather | 今天的天气 | Today's Weather | weather, seasons

It rained hard this morning and I forgot to bring an umbrella.
到 了 中午 天气 就 晴 了， 太阳 很 好。
By noon the weather cleared up and the sun was lovely.
北方 的 秋天 有点 短， 很快 就要 冷 了。
Autumn in the north is a bit short; it will be cold again soon.
我 最 喜欢 秋天， 因为 不 冷 也 不 热。
Autumn is my favorite, because it is neither cold nor hot.
